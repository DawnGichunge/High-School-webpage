Welcome to Horizon High School

Horizon High School is a vibrant learning community dedicated to academic excellence, personal growth, and preparing students for success in college and beyond. Located in a supportive environment, we serve students in grades 9–12 with a rigorous curriculum, dedicated teachers, and a wide range of extracurricular opportunities.

Our mission is to inspire curiosity, build character, and empower every student to reach their full potential. Whether you are a prospective student, parent, or current member of our community, we invite you to explore all that Horizon has to offer.

Why Choose Horizon?

  • Strong college preparatory program with Advanced Placement (AP) courses
  • Award-winning arts, athletics, and STEM clubs
  • Small class sizes and personalized student support
  • Safe, inclusive campus with modern facilities
  • Active parent and community partnerships
