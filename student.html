Student Life at Horizon

Students at Horizon High School are at the center of everything we do. Our campus offers a balanced mix of rigorous academics, creative expression, competitive sports, and leadership opportunities that help young people discover their strengths and passions.

From the moment they enter ninth grade until graduation, Horizon students are encouraged to challenge themselves, support one another, and prepare for the next chapter of their lives with confidence.

Student Organizations & Clubs

  • Student Government Association (SGA)
  • National Honor Society
  • Robotics & STEM Club
  • Drama & Theater Troupe
  • Debate Team
  • Environmental Club
  • Yearbook & Journalism
  • Music Ensembles (Band, Choir, Orchestra)
  • Cultural Awareness Clubs
  • Peer Mentoring Program
